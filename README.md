# The Unwritten Dawn
This is a dependency-free Python 3.12 terminal adventure game. Here the player is named an apprentice storykeeper in an original folklore
inspired village where dawn has been stolen from the future. Recover story fragments, retell them to reshape reality, rebuild trust, and unlock
a hopeful ending.

## How it works
- Use a hybrid interface: numbered narrative choices plus typed commands: `HELP`, `LOOK`, `INVENTORY`, `STORIES`, `TALK`, `RETELL`, `CHECKPOINT`, `RESTART`, and `QUIT`.
- Track player name, current location, collected story fragments, three key relationships, discovered lore, choices, and unlocked checkpoints.
- Let fragments be retold in specific locations or conversations; each retelling alters a scene, opens a route, changes a relationship, or reveals a hidden truth.
- No abrupt failures: poor choices create alternate routes or costs, while still allowing completion.
- Include an in-session checkpoint menu for revisiting unlocked chapters; no files are written.

## Story Structure
- Prologue: the village wakes beneath an unchanging midnight and the player inherits an unfinished story-thread.
- Five explorable locations: the silent market, baobab archive, river of echoes, lantern fields, and the final sealed dawn-gate.
- Gather three core fragments - Courage, Memory, and Mercy - plus optional lore fragments that explain why dawn was taken.
- Resolve three companion arcs through dialogue and retellings; relationships influence the final options.
- Provide three distinct endings, including a best hopeful ending unlocked by all core fragments, sufficient trust, and the central truth, optional lore enriches the ending without blocking completion.
